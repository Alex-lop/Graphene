# A stranger's first hour: how to time one

The one number nobody has is a person's first hour with Graphene. Every walk so far was a model session standing
in for a person. This is how to get a real one, from a friend, a colleague or yourself on a repository you did
not build. It takes 30 to 60 minutes and needs a clock and the first-hour form
(`.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/first-hour.yml`).

## Before

- A person who uses an AI coding assistant, has not seen Graphene, and has a project of their own under git.
- You say nothing about how it works. Give them the README's address and nothing else.
- A timer (a phone) and a note file.

## During: they drive, you watch and write

1. At t=0 they open the README. Write down, in their words, what they say it is.
2. Mark the minute they first run something. Mark every stall: more than 30 seconds without typing, a search,
   a question to you. Do not answer the question; write it down.
3. Mark the minute the demo ends, and the minute a leaf first lands on their own project, if it does.
4. Stop at 60 minutes, or when they stop.

## After: ask, do not lead

Ask the form's six questions in order and write the answers as they say them. Then ask them to file it, or file
it with their words.

## What to keep

Minutes to the first thing that worked; the first stall and what ended it; every word they could not say back;
whether they would use it again. Quote, do not summarize. Do not fix the page during the session.
