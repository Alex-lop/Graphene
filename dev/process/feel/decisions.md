# The feel pass: what was chosen from the 36 findings, and why

A Fable designer read the three readers' findings (`findings.md`) against the source on main 27574d4 and chose
the twelve changes with the largest felt difference for the smallest diff, each with its test and the pins it
moves; what it left out is listed with the reason. A judge then argued each change (its verdict is at the end).
Alex vetoes any line of this: each change is one commit.

## The designer's thesis

Graphene's first hour reads like its own source code. Words: the last or first line of almost every screen is a fact about Graphene's mechanics (`--no-verify`, `left alone, agents can reach`, `red first`, `(the plan of /full/path)`, eight hook names, `finish:`/`stuck:` for an agent who has left) where the person needed the one command to type next. Screen: the status line and the legend spend their 80 columns on separators and the words `critical path`, so `1/9 done`, `? help` and `q quit` fall off the very first screen, and three glyphs (━ ◆ ↩ ·) each mean two things in one box. Rhythm: the two longest waits of the hour, the planner's half-minute and the run's two minutes, are silent in the terminal that started them, while the screen built to show agents at work says `agents 0`.
